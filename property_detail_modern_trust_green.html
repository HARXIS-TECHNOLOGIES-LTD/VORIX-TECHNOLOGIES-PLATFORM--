About This Property

Experience luxury waterfront living in this stunning 3-bedroom apartment located in the prestigious Lekki Phase 1. This modern residence offers breathtaking views of the Lagos lagoon and features contemporary design throughout.

The spacious open-plan living area seamlessly connects the living, dining, and kitchen spaces, perfect for both relaxation and entertaining. Floor-to-ceiling windows flood the space with natural light while showcasing the spectacular water views.

Located in a secure, gated estate with 24/7 security, residents enjoy access to premium amenities including a swimming pool, gym, and landscaped gardens. The property is just minutes away from top shopping centers, international schools, and the bustling business district.

Each bedroom is generously sized with built-in wardrobes, and the master suite features an en-suite bathroom with premium fixtures. The modern kitchen comes equipped with high-end appliances and ample storage space.
